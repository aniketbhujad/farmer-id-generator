Notice for Shopkeepers/Service Providers:

If you are providing cards to farmers through this printing portal:

  • Tell farmers clearly that this is not an official government card.
  • This card is only for storing and organizing farmer ID information.
  • Provide this card only after the farmer understands this and still requests it.
  • The government may issue official cards in the future.

CAUTION: If the government issues an official card, this card will become invalid. Providing incorrect details may lead to legal actions by farmers.
