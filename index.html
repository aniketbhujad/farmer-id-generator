Notice for Service Providers / Shopkeepers:

If you are printing cards (Farmer ID / Ration Card) for citizens through this portal:

  • Inform citizens clearly that this is not an official government card.
  • This card is designed solely for organizing personal records and quick reference.
  • Provide this card only after the citizen fully understands and expressly requests it.

CAUTION: Official government documents take precedence. Misrepresenting unofficial cards as government originals is strictly prohibited.
